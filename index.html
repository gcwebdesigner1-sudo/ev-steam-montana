How it works

Water in. Super-heated dry steam out.

You fill the tank from the kitchen sink. The machine heats it into very hot, very dry "blue" steam that loosens grime and kills germs on contact, then evaporates almost right away.

01

Heat does the scrubbing

At 365°F the steam breaks down grease, soap scum and built-up dirt so you wipe it away instead of scrubbing it.

02

Dry steam, not a soaking

Because the steam is only 3–5% moisture, floors, couches and mattresses are dry in seconds, not hours. No puddles, no mildew.

03

Sanitizes without chemicals

EV states the Atlantis X kills up to 99.9% of bacteria and germs by heat alone. Good news for kids, pets and anyone sensitive to cleaning sprays.

Quick start

Already own one? Here's the basic routine.

This is a quick reminder, not a replacement for the user guide that came in the box. If anything looks off, call Anthony before you keep going.

Watch the full user guide video
  1. Fill with tap water. Use the refill bottle. EV says tap water only, so skip additives, fragrance and vinegar.
  2. Connect the steam hose and click on the attachment you need.
  3. Power on and wait about 5 minutes while the boiler heats up. The screen shows temperature and pressure.
  4. Pick a steam level. Low for fabrics and delicate surfaces, high for grease and grout.
  5. Wrap a towel on the brush (rectangular or triangular pillow) for floors, glass and upholstery so the towel picks up the loosened dirt.
  6. Steam, then wipe. Let the heat do the work. Swap to a clean towel as soon as one gets dirty.
  7. Power down and cool off before you refill or put it away.

Attachment cheat sheet

Which brush for which job?

AttachmentBest for
Rectangular pillow + towelSealed floors, windows, mirrors, larger flat surfaces
Triangular pillow + towelUpholstery, mattresses, corners, cabinets
Round nylon brushes (30 & 60 mm)Grout lines, tile, sinks, everyday scrubbing
Round soft brushMore delicate surfaces where you still want some agitation
Brass or copper / stainless brushesTough baked-on grime on durable surfaces like grill grates and oven racks
Brass tip point nozzleTight spots: faucet bases, vents, seat rails, tracks
Steam plungerSink and tub drains
Extension pipesFloors, high corners and ceilings without bending or climbing

Always test a small hidden spot first. Metal brushes can scratch, so keep them off glass, stone countertops, painted or soft surfaces. Steam isn't recommended for unsealed wood, waxed floors or heat-sensitive plastics. When in doubt, ask Anthony.

Care & maintenance

Keep it running for years.

Tap water only

EV specifies plain tap water. Don't add cleaners, oils or scents to the tank.

Follow the service reminders

The touch screen tracks working hours and service. When it asks for maintenance, follow the steps in your user guide or call us.

Wash your towels

Microfiber and cotton towels do the pickup. Machine-wash them after each session so you aren't spreading dirt around.

Steam cleaning is easier to believe when you see it on your own grout, oven or couch. A demo takes the guesswork out and lets you ask questions about the surfaces you actually have.

How much does it cost?

Is it safe around kids and pets?

The cleaning itself uses only water, so there's no chemical residue left behind. The steam is very hot, though, so keep the nozzle pointed away from people and animals and treat it like any hot appliance.

Will it damage my floors or furniture?

It's built to clean without harming most sealed surfaces, and the low moisture means no soaking. Skip unsealed wood, waxed floors and heat-sensitive materials, and test a hidden spot first. If you're not sure about a surface, ask.
