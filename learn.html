Blue steam technology

Dry steam that cleans, then disappears.

Hotter, higher-pressure steam carries far less water. EV's blue steam is only 3–5% moisture, so it lifts grime and kills germs by heat, then evaporates. No puddles, no soggy carpets.

Quick start & care.

A reminder, not a replacement for the user guide in the box. If anything looks off, call Anthony before you keep going.

Tap water only. No cleaners, oils or scents in the tank.

Follow the service reminders on the touch screen, using your user guide.

Wash your towels after each session so you aren't spreading dirt around.

  1. Fill with tap water using the refill bottle.
  2. Connect the hose and click on the attachment you need.
  3. Power on and wait about 5 minutes. The screen shows temperature and pressure.
  4. Pick a steam level. Low for fabrics, high for grease and grout.
  5. Wrap a towel on the pillow for floors, glass and upholstery.
  6. Steam, then wipe. Swap towels as soon as one gets dirty.
  7. Power down and cool off before you refill or store it.

Questions people ask us.

Who makes the EV Atlantis X?

EV International, a steam-cleaner maker founded in 2013 with production in Italy. More than 200,000 customers worldwide use EV machines. EV of Montana is the local team here, led by Anthony.

Is the demo cleaning really free?

Yes. There's no charge and you don't have to buy anything. The fastest way to show what the Atlantis X can do is to use it on something in your own home.

How much does the Atlantis X cost?

It depends on the package and accessories you choose, so it's covered at your demo or over the phone. Call or text Anthony for current options.

Is it safe around kids and pets?

It cleans with water only, so there's no chemical residue. The steam is very hot, so keep the nozzle pointed away from people and animals like any hot appliance.

Will it damage my floors or furniture?

It's built for most sealed surfaces, and the low moisture means no soaking. Skip unsealed wood, waxed floors and heat-sensitive materials, and test a hidden spot first.
