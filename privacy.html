Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

EV of Montana is a local EV International sales team. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how to reach us about it.

What we collect

We collect what you choose to give us when you request a quote or demo, call, text, email or fill out one of our forms on Facebook or Instagram. That can include your name, phone number, email address, town, what you'd like cleaned, your preferred times and your answers to questions on the form.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your quote, demo or question
  • To schedule and confirm your appointment, including reminders
  • To help you with an EV machine you own
  • To see which of our ads and offers are working

How we share it

We do not sell your information. We share it only with people and services that help us respond to you, such as our team and the marketing partner who manages our ads, or when the law requires it.

Forms on this website

The form on this website does not store your information on the website. It opens a pre-written text or email on your device, and nothing is sent until you press send.

Facebook and Instagram

If you submit a form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, Meta collects that information and makes it available to us. Meta's own privacy policy also applies to your use of its platforms. We may also use Meta's advertising tools to measure visits to this site and show relevant ads to people who have visited it.

Other services on this site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads fonts from Google and images from EV International and YouTube, and plays videos through YouTube. These services may receive technical details such as your IP address when your browser connects to them.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we have about you, or tell us to stop contacting you, at any time. You can reply STOP to any text from us.

Contact us

Email evofmt@gmail.com or call (406) 677-8967.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
